QuoteMetrik Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

This policy explains the data practices of the QuoteMetrik: Invoice & Quote Android app by TejMade Digital (Tej Singh Ranawat). It applies to the current closed-test build. We will update it before introducing new cloud backup, sign-in, or paid-access behavior.

Information you enter

You may enter your business details, customer names and contact information, job addresses, services, prices, quotes, invoices, payment records, expenses, notes, and job photos or receipts. QuoteMetrik stores this information on your device so you can create and manage documents. We do not operate an account or server that receives this workspace data in the current build.

Backups, documents and sharing

You can export a JSON backup and choose where to save it using Android's file picker, including a folder in your own Google Drive if you choose. You can import a selected JSON file to restore data. The current build does not automatically connect to Google Drive or upload your workspace in the background. Your chosen storage provider handles files you place there under its own terms and privacy policy.

When you choose to print or share a PDF, CSV report, or other file, Android passes it to the app or destination you select. Customer-facing PDFs exclude private job expenses and estimated costs. You control which job photos appear in a customer PDF. Optional payment links open an external service you choose; QuoteMetrik records payments manually and does not process customer card payments.

Network activity and third parties

Core document editing works offline. The current build does not show ads or use an analytics service. Google Play and Android system services may process information required to install, update, secure, or run apps under Google's own policies. If you open an external link or share a file with another app, that provider's privacy practices apply.

Retention and your choices

Workspace information remains on your device until you delete individual records or uninstall the app. Android app backup is disabled for this build. A JSON copy you exported remains in the location you selected until you remove it there. Restoring a JSON file replaces local workspace data after confirmation; export a separate copy before restoring. We cannot remotely access or erase data stored only on your device or in your own storage account.

Children

QuoteMetrik is a business tool for adult contractors and is not directed to children.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact sltjranawat@gmail.com. If we change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and its effective date before the changed behavior is released.

Google Drive automatic backup and paid subscriptions are not enabled in the current closed-test build. Any later release that enables them will have an updated privacy policy and Play data declarations.
